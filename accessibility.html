Accessibility

Designed to be usable.

This site uses semantic page structure, visible keyboard focus, a skip link, descriptive links, responsive layouts, and reduced motion preferences. Information is presented in text rather than relying only on visuals or color.

I have checked the basics, but there is more to test. This site has not had a formal Section 508 review. I keep the checks and remaining work in the project’s accessibility notes on GitHub.

If you encounter a barrier, please contact me through LinkedIn and describe the page and the difficulty.
