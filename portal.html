The problem

Team members needed a practical way to track caseload complexity and cycle times and prepare information for performance reviews.

My approach

I designed a performance portal and an interactive database registry to organize the work and support reporting.

Design decisions

I brought tracking and reporting into a shared portal and included an Excel export for appraisal inputs. The structure connected day to day records with the information needed for review.

The result

The portal supported caseload and cycle time tracking and simplified preparation of appraisal inputs through an Excel export.
