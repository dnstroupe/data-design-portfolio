The problem

A recurring leadership reporting process depended on manual preparation of a weekly presentation. Information needed to be consolidated into a view that senior leaders could use.

My approach

I built a Power BI readiness dashboard that brought reporting information together. I worked with leadership and users to translate operational needs into reporting requirements and dashboard designs.

Design decisions

The key change was the reporting format: a consolidated dashboard in place of a manually assembled presentation. I also used an interactive HTML mockup to communicate the proposed architecture during leadership briefings.

The result

The dashboard replaced the manual weekly presentation preparation process. Leadership had a shared view instead of another presentation to assemble.
