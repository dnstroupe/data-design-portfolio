About David Stroupe

Operational experience.
A focus on the person using the tool.

I bring more than 20 years of Department of Defense experience to data visualization, reporting, and operational applications.

My career began in the U.S. Navy, working across personnel programs, administration, and career counseling. That work taught me to turn complicated requirements into information people can act on.

Today, I build operational dashboards and analytical tools at Leidos. I review incoming requests, assess the available data, and develop applications in Palantir Foundry Workshop. My earlier work includes Power BI reporting, Power Automate pipelines, and SharePoint portals.

What guides my work

Moving a process onto a new screen is only worth doing if it makes the work easier. I look at what users need, what information is available, and where the experience can improve.

Developing my design practice

I am enrolled in the Google UX Design certificate course and developing my skills in Figma. My first independent project, Operations Overview, brings together editable Figma layouts, a working dashboard, and notes about the design decisions. My course portfolio projects will be added as I complete them.
