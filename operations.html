Palantir Foundry Workshop · AIP

The problem

The original request was to recreate several SharePoint tracking sites in Foundry. That would have moved the same experience to a different platform. I wanted to understand what the new application could do to make the work easier.

My approach

I started by reviewing the service tickets and checking the datasets and ontology objects already available. Most of the information was there. Where it was missing, it needed to be ingested before I could finish the build. I then looked for related data that could give users a fuller picture. I configured the dashboards myself, working as one of three builders who collaborate but own their individual builds.

Design decisions

I organized the experience around the organization a user needed to review. From that view, they could find related work, overdue tasks, upcoming items across 30, 60, and 90 days, and attached files. I also configured AIP Logic and Augmentation so users could ask questions and get a summary of related information. The aim was to make it easier to move from an overview to the details behind it.

Making it easier to use

I worked through keyboard navigation, focus order, and contrast. I added hover feedback and tooltips to explain controls. I have not completed a formal accessibility review or screen reader testing, so I do not describe these dashboards as fully Section 508 compliant.

What I am working on next

I am developing custom widget prototypes for the two completed dashboards. These explore a more modern interface and bring in additional datasets and objects. They are still in progress.

The result

Two builds have passed user acceptance testing. Two more are in testing, and a readiness dashboard is in development. Feedback on the completed builds was positive, including recognition during a weekly leadership meeting. Requested changes were small, such as button colors and placement. The completed dashboards bring related information into one place to support review and preparation.
